Design proposal · 29 Sep 2026 · v2

One chat, from prompt to playable

It works like any chat assistant. Attach assets and describe the game in one message. Takko says which needed assets it detected in your message and names anything still missing. You supply those by attaching them and pressing Enter, or by asking. While it builds you can keep typing: follow-ups queue and apply after the current step, and Stop is always one click away. This demo starts with two of your three real assets attached, so you can see the missing-asset step.

Dummy Strike

Assets come from the conversation

  • Assets attached to your first message are matched to what the game needs and shown as Detected from your message.
  • Anything missing is named in the reply. Attach it and press Enter, or say "choose for me".
  • Change what's needed in plain words, e.g. "use a straw dummy instead". Takko updates the list.
  • Every asset is checked automatically by the cheap model. ✕ removes one.

Talk to it while it works

  • The composer is never locked. Messages sent during a build are queued and applied after the current step, then marked Applied.
  • While a build runs, the send button becomes Stop when the box is empty. Typing turns it back into Send.
  • The chat keeps full context, so "make it louder" or "not that one" refers to what was just discussed.

Always know what's happening

  • The strip under the header shows one state: Ready, Working, Needs you, Building, Ready to test, Failed or Stopped, plus spend.
  • While building it adds the step, step N of M, elapsed time and Stop.
  • Only the newest card has live buttons. Answered questions shrink to receipts.

Questions only when they matter

  • Asked inline, one at a time, up to three choices plus Other, one Recommended with its reason.
  • The rig is asked first. Your animation is R6, so R6 is recommended and the game uses R6 avatars.
  • Nothing already answered or attached is asked again.
