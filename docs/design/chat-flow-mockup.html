One chat, from prompt to playable

Everything happens in the chat panel. The strip under the header always says what Takko is doing and whether it needs you. Only the newest card asks for anything, and answered cards shrink to one-line receipts. Built from your real run: the three assets you attached and your prompt. Always know what's happening

  • The status strip under the header has one of six states: Ready, Working, Needs you, Building, Ready to test, Stopped or failed.
  • Building shows the current step, step N of M, money spent, time, and a Stop button in the same place every time.
  • The map on the left fills in as steps finish.

One thing to do at a time

  • Only the newest card has live buttons. Older cards shrink to receipts like "Rig: R6".
  • The chat scrolls to the card that needs you. The composer sits under the messages and never covers them.
  • No silent grey buttons. A disabled button always says why, right under it.

Questions in the chat

  • Asked inline, one at a time, up to three choices plus Other, one marked Recommended with its reason.
  • Rig is asked when it matters. Your attached animation is R6, so R6 is recommended and the game is set to R6 avatars.
  • Answers you already gave, or assets you already attached, are never asked for again.

Assets check themselves

  • Attached assets fill the rows straight away. The cheap model reviews scripts and fit on its own, and the result shows as one line.
  • Anything can be removed with ✕. Swapping is Change on the row.
  • Red only for real danger (backdoors, remote code). Everything else is handled or explained.
