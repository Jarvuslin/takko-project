Punch the Dummy: Training Drill

Proposal approved · revision 3

Mechanics

Core loop: the player (default Roblox avatar) faces the selected stationary target dummy asset. Pressing a single 'Punch' input (default: left mouse click or gamepad face button A / keyboard Space) triggers a punch action if the player is not already mid-punch (no combo chaining). The punch action plays a forward jab animation lasting roughly 0.3-0.5 seconds (windup, strike, recovery). A hit is registered when the punch's active strike frame overlaps the dummy's hitbox. On a successful hit: (1) a punch/hit sound effect plays once, (2) the on-screen hit counter increments by exactly 1. A brief cooldown ensures only one hit is registered per punch swing, even if overlap persists across multiple frames. The dummy has no health, cannot be defeated, never attacks back, and shows no flinch or reactive animation. There is no reset-counter control; the counter persists for the play session and resets only on scene reload/restart. Player movement uses standard default Roblox movement controls (WASD/left stick) with no added arena boundaries or movement restrictions beyond the default playable space; no jumping or blocking mechanics are included in this scope.

Assumptions: Single-player only; no multiplayer, AI opponent behavior, or networking. Dummy is purely stationary and cannot be damaged, destroyed, or knocked over, and has no flinch or reactive animation. Only one punch type/animation is required (no combos, kicks, blocks, or special moves) per the request scope. Hit counter is a simple integer displayed as on-screen UI text (e.g., 'Hits: 0') anchored to a screen corner. Counter increments exactly once per successful punch connection using a per-swing hit-registered flag. Default control scheme: standard Roblox movement plus one button for punching; no reset-counter input or button is included. No scoring, timer, win condition, or difficulty progression is required; this is a practice/demo loop. Punch and hit use the same single sound effect (no separate whiff vs. connect sounds) unless later expanded. Player avatar and dummy appearance are provided entirely by the selected default/selected Roblox assets; no custom clothing or rig modifications are required. Camera uses the default Roblox third-person follow camera; no fixed framing or custom camera control is implemented.

Theme

A lighthearted training-gym aesthetic: the player is a boxer/martial artist in practice gear (tank top, wraps or gloves, shorts) working out in a no-frills gym corner. The target dummy is a classic red-and-black canvas heavy-bag-style humanoid dummy mounted on a weighted base, evoking boxing gyms and arcade punch-meter machines. Tone is upbeat and casual, not violent or gory — this is presented as a training/fitness minigame rather than a combat-to-defeat scenario, reinforced by the dummy's harmless flinch reaction and the absence of health bars or damage numbers. Sound design leans into satisfying 'thwack'/glove-impact punch sounds reminiscent of classic arcade boxing games. The on-screen hit counter is styled like a gym rep-counter or arcade score display (bold numerals, simple font) to reinforce the training-drill framing.

Assumptions: Visual tone is stylized/cartoonish rather than photorealistic or gory, keeping the experience family-friendly. Player and dummy character models are generic/unbranded (no licensed characters or IP references). Setting is a single indoor gym-style room; no outdoor or narrative story elements are needed. No dialogue, voiceover, or narrative text is required beyond the hit counter UI. Color palette and lighting are simple/bright (gym lighting) rather than dramatic or moody, matching the low-stakes training theme.

Environment & layout

Layout is a plain flat floor with no additional decoration, props, or themed backdrop. The selected stationary target dummy asset is placed at a fixed point on the floor; the player spawns using the default Roblox avatar a short distance away, within comfortable punching range after a short walk. The on-screen hit counter UI is anchored to a fixed screen-space position (e.g., top-center or top-right) so it remains visible during gameplay. The scene uses the default Roblox camera (third-person follow) with no custom framing. There are no invisible walls, arena boundaries, or added movement restrictions; the player can move freely across the plain floor using standard default movement, relying on default Roblox physics and collision.

Assumptions: Single static scene; no level transitions, multiple rooms, or loading between areas. Play space is a plain floor with no decorative props, backdrop, or themed environment art; only the selected dummy asset and default avatar are present. Lighting uses Roblox default lighting; no custom or dynamic lighting rigs are configured. No destructible or interactive environment props beyond the dummy itself in this scope. No arena boundaries or invisible walls are added; player movement is unrestricted beyond the standard default playable area.
