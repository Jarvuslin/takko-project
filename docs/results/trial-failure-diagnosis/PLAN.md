**Proposed playback contract:** register the exact selected sequence, play its track, then pause/seek with `AdjustSpeed(0)` and `TimePosition`. One click authorizes exactly one segment. Allow at most one additional click edge buffered during a segment. With no buffered edge, clamp to the segment end and hold. No automatic looping or unlimited queued punches. Playback overshoot must clamp before processing any unapproved later hit, and time-crossing logic must emit each authorized hit once even when a render step skips its exact timestamp.

The client owns input, predicted playback and presentation. A server-created Animator supports the character track. The server validates ordered segment requests and combo nonce, maintains its own timing/reset state, and schedules the hit from the accepted segment start plus the measured hit offset. It validates range, facing, line of sight and living targets, and applies at most one hit per segment per target. It never trusts client damage, target selection or track position. Misses do not increment the hit counter. Death/respawn cancels pending hits and invalidates the nonce. Reject/reconcile out-of-order or stale requests. Latency tolerance must be explicit and tested.
