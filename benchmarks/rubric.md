# Game-quality review anchors

Use these anchors for the eleven GameBench dimensions. Intermediate scores are allowed when the reviewer explains the observed difference. **Missing evidence is pending, never an invented zero or pass.** A zero requires an observed failure or absence during a relevant test. DevBench uses its scoped checks instead of this whole-game rubric.

| Dimension (weight)   | 0                                                    | 2                                                              | 5                                                                                   | 8                                                                                               | 10                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mechanics (15)       | Required loop cannot be completed                    | Main action works intermittently; important rules fail         | Complete intended loop with understandable rules; rough edge cases                  | Consistent, responsive rules and meaningful choices, including rejection and recovery paths     | All scoped mechanics and edge cases repeatedly verified; choices reinforce one another exceptionally well                 |
| Content depth (15)   | No playable progression                              | Brief repeated action with no changing decision                | Required progression works, with some meaningful variation and some repetition      | Observed session introduces well-paced decisions, challenges and purposeful replay              | Rich, coherent variation throughout the bounded session; every stage or replay meaningfully develops prior play           |
| Art/environment (15) | Scene unreadable or unusable                         | Placeholder composition; weak scale, silhouettes or navigation | Coherent readable style, landmarks and routes; uneven detail or composition         | Deliberate framing, silhouettes, palette and environmental storytelling support play            | Exceptionally consistent composition and craftsmanship across the assessed scope, with no observed readability compromise |
| Animation (10)       | Required motion absent or broken in play             | Rudimentary or poorly timed motion obscures action             | Readable anticipation, execution and recovery; compatible rig and basic transitions | Expressive, synchronized motion with clean interruption, blending and replication               | Exceptionally clear and consistent motion language; timing, transitions and reactions enhance every assessed action       |
| UI/UX (8)            | Essential interaction or information inaccessible    | Major clipping, confusing state or blocked controls            | Goals, state and actions usable on required devices; minor friction                 | Clear hierarchy, contextual feedback and low-friction navigation across required states/devices | Consistently effortless discovery, error recovery and accessibility throughout the assessed flows                         |
| VFX (7)              | Required event cues absent or misleading             | Generic effects poorly aligned with actual events              | Distinct readable effects for essential accepted/rejected events                    | Cohesive timing, scale and layering reinforce action without clutter                            | Precise, expressive event language remains legible in the busiest assessed situations                                     |
| Audio (5)            | Required sound missing or broken                     | Repetitive, harsh or misleading cues                           | Functional contextual cues with intelligible balance                                | Coherent ambience and event cues with strong spatial/timing clarity                             | Exceptionally intentional mix and dynamic feedback support navigation, mood and action without fatigue                    |
| Game feel (10)       | Interaction consistently frustrating or unresponsive | Weak feedback, timing or camera/control behavior               | Actions feel understandable and reasonably responsive                               | Controls, camera, motion, audiovisual feedback and pacing feel consistently satisfying          | Exceptionally cohesive tactile feedback and timing, supported by repeated human play rather than source claims            |
| Reliability (5)      | Crashes, core softlocks or corrupt state observed    | Frequent failures, leaks or inconsistent client state          | Scoped normal paths stable; a few recoverable issues                                | Repeated sessions and adverse paths stable, including respawn and multiplayer                   | All predeclared normal/adverse paths repeatedly stable with no material observed regression                               |
| Performance (5)      | Required device cannot sustain usable play           | Repeated severe stalls or resource growth                      | Meets declared usable target with occasional hitches                                | Meets declared frame-time/memory targets consistently on matched routes                         | Strong measured headroom and stable resource use across worst-case assessed routes and sessions                           |
| Intent alignment (5) | Requested experience fundamentally missing           | Superficial resemblance; major required features absent        | Requested features and theme represented coherently                                 | Mechanics, presentation and progression strongly deliver the intended experience                | Every scoped requirement is fulfilled with a unified interpretation and no material observed compromise                   |

## Evidence and calibration

Record the artifact hash, evidence IDs, specific observation and reason for each score. Use native gameplay for mechanics and motion, actual audio capture for sound, and matched hardware/route measurements for performance. Object counts, asset IDs, token counts, source code and a model's self-review are diagnostics, not substitutes for these observations. A screenshot can support art assessment but cannot establish animation timing or audio quality.

Before scored reviews, have reviewers independently apply the rubric to the same legally accessible, matched capture segments, then discuss disagreements using the anchors. Blind candidate/model labels and randomize viewing order when feasible. Retain original ratings and disagreements; do not call one uncalibrated opinion objective ground truth. Current catalog references are unreviewed and have no preassigned scores.

Judge relative to the fixed case's scope and required devices, not a commercial game's budget. A score of 10 means exceptional within the assessed scope, not perfection or guaranteed player retention. Use the same declared performance targets for compared candidates; if targets or measurements are missing, performance stays pending.

Hard gates remain separate: a required broken feature, observed core failure or incomplete evidence cannot be averaged away. The weighted total and internal “good-rating eligible” threshold do not establish commercial readiness. Record all failed and incomplete attempts, and keep untouched-model, automated-repair and expert-assisted results separate. Calibrated human review is still required for aesthetic fit, search relevance, game feel and whether long-form play contains meaningful decisions rather than elapsed waiting.
