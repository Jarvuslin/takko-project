# Diversity v1: first run preserved, campaign superseded

The combat-training trial made two paid calls and charged **$0.032386**. No budget cancellation, manual rescue, native import, generated artifact or finished game. [Raw results](combat-training/results.json), [raw plan](combat-training/plan-project.json), [model trace](combat-training/model-trace.json).

Gemini produced an accepted schema-valid plan with a humanoid training dummy need and two sound needs. Grok chose a dummy from twenty returned Creator Store candidates and grounded its reason in dummy health, combo hit reactions and requirements. However, the plan proposed procedural Motor6D motion without a distinct complete-combat/animation component search. That is incomplete Marketplace-first coverage, even though the plan retained the major combat requirements. This single observation does not establish that a worker fully understands the game or can integrate a complete combat component.

The pipeline rejected the exact returned candidate before native import with `Candidate was not returned by this adapter's scoped search`. Diagnosis: the adapter appends optional `description` after other fields, while Zod reconstructs candidates with description near the start. JSON.stringify comparison incorrectly treated reordered keys as different metadata. This is a product regression, not a fabricated asset choice or a dollar-cap failure.

Stopped the version after this deterministic bug instead of charging for the same known failure across two more genres. Settings restored; no pending calls or in-flight reservations. The empty-receipt pre-dispatch error conservatively flagged reconciliation and the controller retained its lock. A separate [read-only native reconciliation](combat-training/reconciliation.json) confirms the project's scope is absent throughout the Edit datamodel; historical results retain their original flag. Historical campaign totals now $0.543290 charged and $4.739984 cumulative reservations. A separate v2 changes only the generic identity comparison to deep field equality, rejecting changed or extra metadata while accepting different object-key order. It reuses the same frozen prompts in new projects, not this failed plan or selected asset.
