# Butter Crunch pilot: planning gate failed

After the user enabled setup, all four profile keys were loaded and the application-owned MCP connection found disposable Place1. Three independent Gemini 3.7 Flash plans were requested for the intended MiniMax M3, MiMo V2.5 Pro and Grok Build trials. Four billable planner calls occurred, including one automatic format/validation correction. Total provider-reported charge: **$0.052133**.

| Intended builder | External plan evaluation | Builder executed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MiniMax M3 | Required sound was marked optional; strategy proposed unverified procedural/built-in replacements. | No |
| MiMo V2.5 Pro | Required sound was marked optional; strategy proposed stock/synthesized fallback audio. | No |
| Grok Build 0.1 | Audio was required, but visual Marketplace sourcing was absent from executable asset needs. | No |

The pre-run protocol requires rejecting plans that do not meet the frozen brief. Astra evaluated these plans and did not rewrite them, approve them, change the prompt, select/import assets or author game code. The app itself accepted these plans into its `review` state: the failure is recorded in this external benchmark report, not fabricated as an application-generated failure state.

This reveals a **Takko planning validation gap**, not comparative worker performance. The application validates presence and identity of asset needs but does not enforce the required sourcing intent. Neither static plan acceptance nor a claimed fallback guarantees actual sound or Marketplace execution. No worker model, asset loop, generated artifact or native gameplay test ran. There are **zero finished games and no quality ranking**.

The original public routes and budget settings were restored; all loaded keys were preserved in memory. Model profiles and draft plans remain available for inspection. Do not restart the app and lose the keys. Snapshots, hashes, costs and classifications are in `results.json`. The original `experiment.json` and preflight files are historical setup records; this result supersedes their setup-blocked status.

Any future planning validation change or alternate planner must be a separate versioned run. Preserve these failures. Do not fix their plans or relabel them as successful unassisted generations.
