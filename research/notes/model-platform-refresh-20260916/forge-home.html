Frequently Asked Questions

What is ForgeGUI and who is it for?
ForgeGUI is an AI-powered tool that helps game developers generate high-quality game assets like thumbnails, icons, GUI sets, GFX, and more. It's built for Roblox developers, UEFN creators, and anyone who needs professional game assets fast.
Is ForgeGUI free?
Yes! ForgeGUI offers a free tier with starter credits so you can try it out. Premium plans unlock more credits, faster generation, and advanced features. Try it for free, cancel any time.
What are credits and how do I use them?
Credits are used each time you generate an asset. Different generation types cost different amounts of credits. You receive credits when you sign up, and can earn more through referrals or by subscribing to a plan.
Where else can I use ForgeGUI?
ForgeGUI works great for any game platform — Roblox, Fortnite (UEFN), Minecraft, Unity, Unreal Engine, mobile, and more. Export your assets as PNGs, ZIPs, GLBs and use them anywhere.
What is the license for assets generated by ForgeGUI?
Assets created using ForgeGUI are yours. You hold a license with full commercial use rights, along with permission to use, modify, distribute or sell these assets in your games.
Can I use my own assets as reference?
Yes — the style reference system lets you upload any screenshot or image. The AI analyzes the color palette, lighting, and style and applies it to everything it generates, keeping all your assets cohesive.
Are my ForgeGUI generations added to the public Asset Catalog?
Eligible generations made with free credits or during a free trial may be added to ForgeGUI's public Asset Catalog after you accept the catalog notice. Public assets can be viewed, downloaded, and indexed by search engines. You can stop future publication in My Account → Publish free generations. Turning the toggle off cancels pending publication, but does not automatically remove assets already published. To remove existing catalog entries, use My Account → Published assets → Remove all. Paid-plan generations are private by default unless you opt in, and you retain ownership of your generations.
